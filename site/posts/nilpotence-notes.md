I have just finished giving an Algebraic Topology 3 course at University of Oslo. Lecture notes are available [here](/files/nilpotence.pdf). They cover roughly the following:

* Some elementary stable homotopy theory
* Spectral sequences
* The Adams spectral sequence
* MU and its homotopy groups
* Synthetic spectra
* The Nishida nilpotence theorem
* Burklund's proof of the Devinatz-Hopkins-Smith nilpotence theorem
* The thick subcategory and periodicity theorems
